These documents describe the problems, solvers, and comparison metrics included
in the library.

- [Problems](problem.md): unconstrained, constrained, multi-objective, dynamic
  multi-objective, and moving peaks benchmarks.
- [Solvers](solver.md): PSO, GA, DE, MGPSO, and the co-evolutionary Lagrangian
  solver (CCLS/CCPSO).
- [Comparison Metrics](compare.md): IGD, GD, hypervolume, hypervolume ratio,
  spread, and P_RED.
